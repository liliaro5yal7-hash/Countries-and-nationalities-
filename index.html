Traditional Games Around the World

🇨🇳

Go (Weiqi)

China • Chinese

An ancient strategy board game for two players. Originating in China over 2,500 years ago, it is renowned for its deep strategic complexity despite simple rules.

🇯🇵

Shogi

Japan • Japanese

Often called Japanese Chess, Shogi is unique because captured pieces can be "dropped" back onto the board as your own, leading to incredibly dynamic and complex gameplay.

🇫🇷

Pétanque

France • French

A beloved outdoor boules game where players throw heavy metal balls as close as possible to a small wooden target ball. A staple of French village squares.

🇪🇹

Mancala

Ethiopia / Africa • Ethiopian

Known as the world's oldest game, Mancala involves sowing and capturing seeds in pits. It has hundreds of regional variations across the African continent.

🇲🇾

Sepak Takraw

Malaysia • Malaysian

Often described as "kick volleyball", players use their feet, knees, chest, and head to keep a rattan ball in the air over a net. Highly athletic and acrobatic.

🇮🇪

Hurling

Ireland • Irish

One of the world's fastest field games, dating back over 3,000 years. Players use a wooden stick (hurley) to hit a small ball (sliotar) between goalposts.
